Tips for the video maker

Shots to take
  • Opening: the host in front of the school gate or signboard
  • Short thank you message from the stakeholder, with their permission
  • The activity, the gifts or repairs, and the finished work
  • Learners saying "Thank you!" together
  • Closing: the host with learners and stakeholders
Good video habits
  • Hold the phone sideways (landscape)
  • Keep it between 1 and 3 minutes
  • Record in a bright and quiet place
  • Speak slowly and smile
  • Follow your school's child protection and media consent rules for learners
